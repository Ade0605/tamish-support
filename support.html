Common questions

Where is my family's information stored?

Only on your own iPhone, in the app's private storage. There is no Tamish server, so we can't see, recover or hand over your data. That's why backups matter (see below).

Can I use Tamish on more than one phone?

Not yet. Sharing between family members' phones isn't available. Each phone keeps its own copy. You can move your data to a new phone with a backup.

How do I back up, and move to a new phone?

An admin opens the Family tab, goes to Backup, and chooses a passphrase of at least 8 characters (a long phrase is safer). Save the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install Tamish, sign in, and choose Restore in the same place. If you lose the passphrase, nobody can open the backup, including us.

I lost my phone. Can I get my data back?

Only from a backup you made. Restore it on the new phone with its passphrase. Because your phone is locked by Face ID or a passcode, the data on the lost phone is protected. Erase it remotely with Find My, and change any passwords that were in the Vault.

How do I delete everything?

Family tab, then Privacy, then "Delete all my data on this device". It removes every record, the Vault key, saved settings and scheduled reminders, and signs you out. Deleting the app also removes the data. To stop using Sign in with Apple with Tamish, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Sign-In & Security.

What can teens and guests see?

Teens can't see money, bills, subscriptions, the Vault, documents, health or insights. Guests can look at shared lists, the calendar and similar, but can't change anything. Only an admin can see and change the family's money.

How does Kid Mode work?

In the Family tab choose "Start Kid Mode" for a child. The phone then shows only that child's chores, stars and rewards. A grown-up leaves with Face ID or the passcode.

Why didn't I get a reminder or an arrival alert?

Check iPhone Settings, Notifications, Tamish, and Location. Reminders and alerts depend on those settings and on the phone's signal, so please don't rely on them for safety. Bill reminders only appear for unpaid bills that have a date.

Why is a bill still showing as overdue?

A bill you didn't tick as paid stays on the list until you do, so a missed bill doesn't disappear on the 1st. Tick it in the Dashboard tab under pending bills.

My Face ID lock isn't working.

Make sure Face ID is set up on the iPhone and that Tamish is allowed to use it in Settings, Face ID & Passcode. You can unlock with the iPhone passcode instead.

Report a problem or a security concern

Please report it privately using this private form, not in a public post. Please give us reasonable time to fix a problem before telling anyone else.
