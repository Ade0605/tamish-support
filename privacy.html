Tamish Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

The short version

Tamish keeps your family's information on your own device. There is no Tamish server, so nobody at Tamish can see it, sell it, or hand it over.

We don't run ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't use analytics.

What the app stores

Only what you or your family type in or choose: names and photos of family members and children, the family picture Gallery, calendar events, chores, shopping lists, messages, meal plans, timetables, budgets and subscriptions, health and emergency contacts, vehicles, pets, trips, goals, saved places, notes, saved passwords in the Vault, and documents you add.

Some of this is sensitive, such as money, health, passwords and children's details. The app treats it that way: money, passwords and documents are limited to family admins. Health records can be viewed by Members but only changed by admins, and Teens and Guests can't see them.

Where it lives

On this device, in the app's private storage. The family's records are left out of iPhone and iCloud backups on purpose, so deleting the app deletes them. A few small settings, such as your lock choice, can be included in a normal iPhone backup.

Sharing between family members' phones isn't available yet. Before it is, this policy will be updated and the app will tell you what will leave your device.

How it's protected

The app locks behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, and hides its screen in the app switcher.

Vault passwords and documents are encrypted with a key that is kept in this device's Keychain and never leaves it.

Changes to money and medical records are written to an activity log on the device.

Permissions the app may ask for

Location: to show saved places on a map and send arrival and leaving alerts. Sharing your own location is a choice you can switch off.

Notifications: for reminders, the daily summary and alerts. They are created on your device.

Calendar: optional. If you turn it on, family events are copied into your iPhone's Calendar app. You can also choose calendars to copy events from: only titles and times are copied, and only after you've seen what will be added.

Photos: only the pictures you pick. The app can't see the rest of your library.

Face ID: to unlock the app and open the Vault.

You can change any of these at any time in the iPhone's Settings app. The app still works without them.

Other services involved

Sign in with Apple: the app keeps only the anonymous identifier Apple gives it, to remember that you're signed in. It doesn't keep your email address.

Apple Maps: map views are drawn by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Siri and Shortcuts: if you use voice commands, what you say is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Tamish only receives the request, such as an item to add. Spoken summaries of the day leave out money, and these commands only work while your iPhone is unlocked and the app lock is open, and they're switched off in Kid Mode.

Home Screen widget: if you add the Family widget, it shows your next event and number of chores on the Home Screen, where anyone holding your unlocked phone can see it. Remove the widget to stop that. Kid Mode clears it.

Google Sheets: only if an admin pastes a link to a household budget sheet. The app then downloads that sheet from Google, so Google can see that request. Nothing else is sent, and the link is removed when you sign out or delete your data.

Children

Children don't have accounts. A parent or guardian enters information about them. It is used only to run the family's own lists and calendars, never for advertising or profiling, and it can be deleted at any time.

Backups you make

An admin can save a backup file protected by a passphrase they choose. It also holds the key to your saved passwords and documents, so a new phone can open them after a restore. It is theirs to keep wherever they like. Nobody else can open it, and a forgotten passphrase can't be recovered.

Your choices

Delete everything: in the Family tab, under Privacy, "Delete all my data on this device" removes every record, the Vault key, saved settings (except your app lock choice) and scheduled reminders, and signs you out. It can't be undone, so make a backup first if you want one.

Sign out: also clears this device's family data.

Events already copied into the iPhone Calendar app stay there until you remove them in that app.

To stop using Sign in with Apple with this app, go to Settings, your name, then Sign-In & Security.

How long we keep it

For as long as the app is on your device and you haven't deleted it. We hold no copy.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so. For questions, write to adebanjoa@rocketmail.com or see the support page at https://ade0605.github.io/tamish-support/support.html.
