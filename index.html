Your family's day, in one private app.

Calendar, chores with star rewards, meals, bills and budget, messages, a family map and a photo gallery. All kept on your own iPhone. No ads. No tracking. No Tamish server.

What it does

  • Plan the week. A clear calendar, a home screen for today, a meal planner with a one-tap grocery list, shared shopping lists.
  • Chores kids do. Stars for every chore, a family leaderboard, Kid Mode with a child-friendly screen, and a pocket money tracker.
  • Bills and budget, calm. Charts, pending and regular monthly bills, reminders that never show amounts on the lock screen.
  • Stay connected. A private family messenger, a map with saved places, and a shared photo Gallery.
  • Keep things safe. Face ID lock, an encrypted Vault for passwords and documents, encrypted backups, and roles for Admin, Member, Teen and Guest.
Private by design. Your information stays on your device. We can't see it, sell it or hand it over. You can delete everything on the phone whenever you like. Read the privacy policy.
